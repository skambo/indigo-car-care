Mobile Detailing Explained

What Is Mobile Detailing?

To most people, a car just gets washed. But a standard car wash in Kenya only removes loose surface dirt — often leaving behind fine scratches, swirl marks, and deep-seated grime in your seats.

Mobile Detailing is an intensive, top-to-bottom restoration and protection process for your vehicle, delivered to your location. Instead of driving to a car wash and leaving your car for days, Indigo brings the entire studio to you. We use specialised machine tools, safe chemical treatments, and deep-cleaning extraction machines to bring your car's interior and exterior back to showroom condition — right where it is parked. We bring our own water and power.
