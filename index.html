The Science

Why Dry Ice Blasting?

Traditional engine bay and undercarriage cleaning relies on high-pressure water, which risks shorted sensors, rust on unpainted metal, and damaged wiring harnesses. Indigo's dry ice blasting uses solid CO₂ pellets at −78.5°C. On impact, the pellets sublimate instantly, creating a micro-thermal shock that lifts caked oil, grease, carbon, and grime without ever wetting a surface.

100% Waterless & Non-Conductive
