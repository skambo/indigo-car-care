Your Ride, Your Pride — Edition 1

Welcome to Your Ride, Your Pride

19 June 2026 · Joe, Founder — Indigo Car Care Club

Hello, everyone!

I'm Joe, the founder of the Indigo Car Care Club, and I'm thrilled to welcome you to our inaugural edition of Your Ride, Your Pride! This newsletter is designed for those who take absolute pride in their vehicles and want to keep them looking their very best. Whether you're a seasoned car enthusiast, a premium car dealership looking to optimise inventory value, or an owner determined to preserve your vehicle's factory finish, you're in the right place!

At Indigo, we believe that every car tells a story, and we want to help you protect yours. Our community is focused entirely on sharing expert tips, chemical insights, and the modern trends shaping premium auto detailing and reconditioning.

What to Expect Each Edition

  • Expert Tips: From protecting your clear coat against intense tropical UV rot to keeping your interior spotless, our articles provide clinical, actionable advice to elevate your maintenance standard.
  • Product Insights: We'll break down the science behind premium surface care, helping you make informed choices for your vehicle. Explore our full catalogue of specialised formulas at indigocarcare.com/products.
  • Exclusive Perks & Discounts: As an active member of the Indigo Car Care Club, you'll unlock priority access to car shows, auto conventions, and private offers on our premium car care product lines — proudly formulated in-house to elite international quality standards.
  • Spotlight on Services: We deliver uncompromised value through our top-tier mobile detailing services, operating directly across Nairobi and Kisumu. Our dedicated team brings world-class automotive reconditioning straight to you, allowing you to watch the transformation unfold from the absolute comfort of your patio.

As we embark on this journey together, I encourage you to engage with us. Share your detailing questions, your preservation challenges, and connect with a community that refuses to settle for average maintenance.

Thank you for anchoring yourself with the Indigo Car Care Club. I am incredibly excited to share this execution blueprint with you.

Here's to ownership, sovereignty, and the long journey ahead!

Happy detailing, — Joe
Founder, Indigo Car Care Club
